What is a Prime Number?

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that has exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself.

Examples of prime numbers include 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 and 13.

The number 1 is not considered a prime number.

How to Use

1. Enter a whole number in the input box.

2. Click "Check Prime Number".

3. The calculator will tell you whether the number is prime or composite.
